Most pavement failures start before the potholes

Small surface cracks let water below the asphalt. Freeze thaw cycles then work on the base from underneath, and the structure weakens before anything visible happens on top. By the time you see widespread cracking, pooling water or loose aggregate, the deterioration usually covers more area than it appears to.

Timing is the whole game. Early maintenance extends pavement life at a fraction of the cost of replacement. The following are the signs that the window is closing.

  • Cracks are spreading past isolated surface areas
  • Water still sits on the pavement hours after rain
  • Edges are breaking down or crumbling under traffic
  • Parking lot traffic patterns have become unclear or unsafe
  • Small repairs are getting more frequent year over year
  • Sections of pavement are sinking or separating

Maintenance or structural?

Not every pavement problem takes the same solution, and treating them the same is how property owners end up paying for a fix that fails early. Traffic volume, drainage performance, sub base stability, surface age and the pattern of cracking all decide which category a surface falls into.

If sealcoating or patching will extend the life of your surface cost effectively, we will tell you that instead of selling a full repave.

What we do to pavement

Asphalt sealcoating

Sealcoating is the most cost effective maintenance step available for any asphalt surface. It slows UV oxidation, repels water and petrochemicals, and restores the dark finish. In Saratoga Springs, where the freeze thaw cycle runs hard from November through March, an unsealed driveway or lot loses its binder considerably faster than it would in a milder climate.

We apply asphalt emulsion sealer to residential driveways and commercial lots throughout Saratoga Springs, Wilton, Malta and Ballston Spa. Every job gets the surface cleaned and prepped, active cracks filled, and two coats for full, even coverage. Cure time runs 24 to 48 hours depending on temperature and humidity.

Line striping and ADA layout

Clear pavement markings protect the property and reduce liability exposure. Faded lines cut usable capacity and create traffic confusion, and once you restripe, current 2010 ADA standards apply to the new layout. There is no grandfathering.

  • Parking stall layout, new construction and restripe
  • ADA compliant accessible spaces and access aisles
  • Van accessible stalls with 8 foot access aisles
  • Fire lane striping and red curb painting
  • Directional arrows, stop markings and custom stenciling
  • Restriping after sealcoating or resurfacing

Striping needs dry pavement above 50 degrees Fahrenheit, which puts the practical window at May through September. We sequence it against sealcoat jobs so there is no long gap between crews.

Asphalt paving and resurfacing

New installation or resurfacing is a larger investment than routine maintenance, and the right moment to act is before a surface passes the point where repair still makes sense. Surface oxidation and minor cracking respond to sealcoating. Alligator cracking, deep potholes and base failure require paving.

  • New asphalt installation for driveways, parking lots and access roads
  • Asphalt overlay over structurally sound existing pavement
  • Pothole patching and repair
  • Crack filling and hot rubberized sealing
  • Asphalt milling
  • Pulverizing and full depth reclamation for severely deteriorated surfaces

Why the sequence matters

Crack filling, then sealcoating, then striping. Run out of order, sealer goes over open cracks and new paint goes under a coat that has not cured. Running all three under one contract is what keeps the order right.

Paving questions we get in Saratoga Springs

How do I know whether my lot needs sealcoating, resurfacing or full repaving?

Sealcoating works on structurally sound asphalt with surface oxidation and minor cracking. Resurfacing, meaning an overlay, addresses heavier surface deterioration but still requires a solid sub base. Full repaving is the answer once the base itself has failed, which usually shows up as widespread alligator cracking, sinking sections, or potholes that keep returning in the same place. We assess surface and base condition on every estimate.

How often should a commercial parking lot be sealcoated?

Every two to three years in Saratoga County. Sealing annually causes buildup and peeling, because the material cannot bond to a surface that was just sealed. Waiting past three years lets oxidation and freeze thaw damage move faster than maintenance can keep up with. High traffic lots belong at the two year end of that range.

When does a parking lot need restriping?

Restripe when stall boundaries are unclear in midday light, after any sealcoating or resurfacing, after a layout change, or when an accessibility audit flags your accessible stalls. Most commercial lots here need it every one to three years depending on traffic and sun exposure. Note that restriping triggers current 2010 ADA standards. Older layouts are not grandfathered.

What is the lifespan of an asphalt parking lot in upstate New York?

A properly installed and maintained asphalt lot typically lasts 20 to 30 years before full replacement. Sealcoating on a two to three year cycle and prompt crack repair are the two steps that move that number the most.

What is the best time of year to pave or sealcoat here?

Late May through September. Both asphalt work and sealcoating need sustained temperatures above 50 degrees Fahrenheit during application and cure. We do not apply sealer or lay new asphalt when rain is forecast inside 24 hours, or when overnight lows are expected below 50 degrees.

Can you combine crack repair, sealcoating and striping in one visit?

Yes, and for a commercial property it is the efficient way to run it. The sequence is crack filling first, then sealcoating, then line striping once the sealer has cured, typically 24 to 48 hours later. One mobilization, one invoice, minimal disruption.
