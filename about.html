One contractor, the whole sequence

Most exterior projects need more than one trade. Running them separately is where schedules slip and accountability gets blurry.

  1. Clear and excavate. Trees, brush and debris off the lot, then the dig for foundations, basements, septic or utility runs, taken at or below frost depth.
  2. Trench and connect. Water, sewer, electrical and drainage lines set at code depth, with 811 called before the first bucket goes in the ground.
  3. Grade. Rough grade to establish elevation, then finish grade so water leaves the property instead of pooling against it.
  4. Pour and pave. Concrete flatwork and structural pours, or asphalt installation and overlay, on a base that was actually compacted.
  5. Seal and stripe. Crack fill, sealcoat, then line striping once the sealer has cured, to current ADA standards where they apply.
  6. Maintain. Sealcoat on a two to three year cycle, restripe as needed, and plow and salt through the winter.
