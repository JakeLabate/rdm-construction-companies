Service index

Grouped the way the work actually sequences on a site, from the ground up.

Asphalt and pavement

  • Asphalt paving and resurfacingNew installation, overlays, pothole patching and crack sealing for driveways, lots and access roads.
  • Residential driveway pavingExcavation, compacted stone base and hot mix asphalt at 2.5 to 3 inches, plus widening and turnaround pads.
  • Asphalt sealcoatingTwo coat asphalt emulsion sealer on a two to three year cycle, with crack filling first.
  • Line striping and ADA layoutStall layout, accessible spaces and aisles, fire lanes, arrows and stenciling to current 2010 standards.
  • Asphalt millingRemoving a controlled depth of surface asphalt to correct grade before an overlay.
  • Pulverizing and full depth reclamationGrinding a failed surface and its base into new compacted base material in place.

Concrete

  • Concrete installation and repairDriveways, sidewalks, walkways and curbing poured on a compacted gravel base with proper control joints.
  • Patios, steps, stoops and landingsBroom finished, stamped and decorative slabs, plus structural steps tied to foundations.
  • Foundation slabs and basement floorsGarage floors, utility pads, basement slab replacement and structural pours for new construction.
  • Commercial concreteADA compliant walkways, curbs and ramps, storefront improvements and new build site work.

Excavation and earthwork

  • Excavation and trenchingFoundation, basement, footing and septic excavation, plus utility and drainage trenches to code depth.
  • Rough and finish gradingElevation, drainage swales, driveway base prep and final surface contours.
  • Lot and site clearingBrush, tree and stump removal, structure demolition, debris haul off and lot leveling.
  • EV charging station site prepPad excavation and conduit trenching for charging infrastructure.

Water, sewer and drainage

  • Water line installation and replacementNew service lines and replacements set below the 48 inch frost depth with proper bedding and backfill.
  • Sewer line installation and replacementFull replacement, laterals to the municipal connection and septic excavation coordination.
  • French, channel and storm drainsPerforated pipe in gravel trenches, surface channel systems and storm drainage for runoff control.
  • Perimeter foundation and yard drainageFooting drains that cut hydrostatic pressure, plus yard systems for chronic standing water.
  • Catch basins and dry wellsInstallation and maintenance of catch basins, plus dry wells for on site stormwater infiltration.

Structure and landscape

  • Retaining wall design and installationPoured concrete, segmental block, natural stone and timber, engineered with drainage behind the wall.
  • Retaining wall repair and replacementAssessment of leaning, bulging or separating walls, then repair or rebuild depending on the base.

Winter service

  • Commercial snow plowingSeasonal contracts with documented trigger depths, staged plowing and pre dawn scheduling.
  • Residential plowingDriveways of every configuration, including long rural runs, plus walks, steps and entries.
  • De-icing, salting and pre-treatmentPost storm salting included, calcium and magnesium chloride below 15 degrees, liquid pre-treatment on request.
  • Snow removal and haulingLoading and hauling snow off site once a property runs out of storage capacity.
