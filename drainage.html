Water line services

Water lines in Saratoga County have to be buried at a minimum of 48 inches to sit below the frost line. Lines installed too shallow freeze and rupture through a Capital Region winter, and the repair is an emergency excavation in frozen ground.

RDM installs new water service lines and replaces failing ones with proper depth, bedding and backfill.

Sewer line services

Drainage systems

Poor drainage is one of the most common and most damaging property problems in the Capital Region. Saratoga Springs takes roughly 45 inches of precipitation a year, and the spring snowmelt concentrates a large share of it into a few weeks. Properties without adequate systems get chronic basement moisture, erosion, standing water and landscape damage.

French drains

Perforated pipe installed inside gravel filled trenches to intercept groundwater and redirect it away from foundations, yards and landscaped areas. This is the right system for soggy lawns, basement moisture, erosion, or poor drainage that shows up after heavy rain. It works below the surface, on water that is already in the ground.

Channel drains

Surface level linear systems that capture and move water off hardscape quickly: driveways, patios, garage aprons and parking areas. They prevent standing water, reduce slip hazards, and protect concrete and asphalt from long term water damage. Where a French drain handles groundwater, a channel drain handles runoff before it has anywhere to soak in.

Storm drain installation

Systems sized to control heavy rainwater runoff and direct it safely away from structures, paved surfaces and low lying areas. Residential and commercial. Storm drainage is what keeps a large impervious area, like a parking lot, from turning every heavy rain into a flooding event at the low corner.

Perimeter foundation drains

Installed around the base of a structure to move groundwater away from the foundation, reduce hydrostatic pressure and cut the risk of basement leaks and foundation cracking. Especially important on properties with poor soil drainage or a history of basement water.

Yard and site drainage

For properties with chronic standing water, muddy areas or erosion, we design systems that move excess water away from lawns, landscaping, foundations and outdoor living areas. Often this is a grading correction as much as a piped system, and we handle both.

Catch basins and dry wells

A catch basin is an inlet with a grate and a sump below it that collects surface water and feeds it into a drainage line, while the sump traps sediment and debris before they reach the pipe. That sump is also why catch basins need maintenance: once it fills, the basin stops working and starts backing up. We install new basins and clean and maintain existing ones.

A dry well is the opposite approach. Rather than moving water off site, it holds it in a buried chamber surrounded by stone and lets it infiltrate into the soil over time. It is the right answer where there is nowhere to discharge to, and where soil percolation is good enough to accept the volume. We install dry wells for roof leader discharge, driveway runoff and yard drainage.

Which system for which problem

Water already in the ground and surfacing in the lawn or the basement: French drain or perimeter foundation drain.

Water running off a hard surface with nowhere to go: channel drain or catch basin into a storm line.

Water with no legal or practical discharge point: dry well, if the soil will take it.

Water arriving because the ground slopes the wrong way: regrading first, then a system if it is still needed. Piping around a grade problem is more expensive than fixing the grade.

Spring is diagnostic

If you want to know what a property's drainage actually does, walk it in late March. Snowmelt puts every fault on display at once.

Water, sewer and drainage questions

How deep do water lines need to be in Saratoga Springs?

Water lines in Saratoga County must be buried at a minimum of 48 inches, below the local frost line, to prevent freezing and rupture. Lines set shallower than that will freeze during a Capital Region winter. We install to code required depth and use appropriate bedding and backfill to protect the line long term.

What is a French drain and when do I need one?

A French drain is a perforated pipe set in a gravel filled trench that intercepts groundwater or surface runoff and redirects it away from a structure or off the property. It is the right solution when water is consistently pooling near a foundation, in a low area of the yard, or along a driveway. Channel drains are better for hardscape surfaces where surface water needs to be caught and moved quickly.

Do I need a permit for water or sewer line work?

Yes, in most cases. Water and sewer line installation and replacement in the City of Saratoga Springs requires permits from the city and coordination with the Saratoga County Water Authority or the municipal sewer department. We identify the requirements for your project and coordinate approvals before work begins.

How long does water or sewer line installation take?

A standard residential water or sewer line replacement typically takes one to three days depending on length, depth, site conditions, and how quickly utility locates and permits are processed. We give a realistic timeline during the estimate.

What is the difference between what RDM does and what a plumber does?

RDM is an excavation and construction contractor. We handle underground water and sewer lines, drainage system installation, and the excavation that goes with it. A plumber handles in-home pipe work: fixtures, repairs, water heaters and interior connections. Many projects need both, with us installing the underground service line and a plumber connecting it inside.

Can you handle drainage problems that get worse in spring?

Yes, and spring is when we hear from most property owners about standing water. Snowmelt concentrates a large volume of water into a short period, and any property without proper drainage or grading shows the problem clearly in March and April. We assess the site to find the source and install the right system for it.
